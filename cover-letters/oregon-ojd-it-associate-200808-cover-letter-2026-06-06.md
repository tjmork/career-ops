**To the Hiring Manager,**

I am applying for the **Information Technology Associate** position supporting **Clackamas County Circuit Court**. I am an **Oregon resident** (Hillsboro / Portland metro), a **U.S. Army National Guard veteran**, and motivated to bring dependable technical support to a court environment where reliability, clear communication, and calm troubleshooting directly affect public access to justice.

My background combines **production systems support**, **client-facing technical communication**, and **documentation** across enterprise settings. I hold a **BS in Computer Information Systems** and more than **ten years** of professional technical experience. Below I address each **Requested Skill and Attribute** as required.

**Problem analysis and solutions.** I routinely diagnose complex production issues spanning applications, data feeds, and scheduled jobs—evaluating logs, isolating root causes, and implementing fixes under time pressure. At Bath & Body Works I owned severity-1 customer and store data paths; at National Data Systems I refactored onboarding logic and SQL access to resolve performance and reliability problems affecting B2B clients.

**Customer service across skill levels.** I have served as a **primary client liaison** for an at-risk enterprise account, explained technical constraints to non-technical stakeholders, and supported store and operations partners during integrations. I am comfortable communicating in person, by phone, email, and chat, adjusting detail and tone for judges, staff, or vendors with varying technical comfort.

**A/V equipment setup and troubleshooting.** **Courtroom A/V has not been the primary focus of my recent roles.** I have supported **presentation and meeting technology** in enterprise settings and am prepared to learn OJD’s courtroom standards quickly. I take seriously the requirement to respond in real time during court events and would prioritize hands-on training and clear runbooks for each courtroom configuration.

**Microsoft Windows enterprise environment.** I have worked in **Windows Server** environments (IIS, RDP, scheduled tasks), supported **Windows-based tooling** for development and operations teams, and troubleshot connectivity, permissions, and peripheral issues in business settings. I am comfortable maintaining desktops and laptops as part of broader infrastructure support.

**Installing and configuring Windows OS in a business setting.** I have configured and maintained Windows systems for development, staging, and server workloads in enterprise contexts, including imaging/rebuild scenarios coordinated with infrastructure partners.

**Windows Server.** I have direct experience with **Windows Server**, IIS, RDP access, and scheduled task automation in production support workflows, including server separation and migration coordination noted in recent enterprise work.

**Active Directory (preferred).** **Dedicated Active Directory administration has not been the center of my recent role**, but I understand identity, access, and account lifecycle concepts from OAuth, Okta, and enterprise auth integrations. I am motivated to deepen hands-on AD skills in a court IT setting.

**Group Policy (preferred).** I have not owned Group Policy administration at scale; I am prepared to learn OJD standards and pair with senior staff during rollout and troubleshooting.

**iOS devices in an enterprise environment (preferred).** At Saks OFF 5TH I **coordinated iOS app releases** with engineering and Apple App Store processes—build pipelines, TestFlight/Bitrise, and production validation across web and mobile. That experience translates to supporting managed mobile devices, updates, and user-facing troubleshooting.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my support discipline, documentation habits, and public-service motivation can help Clackamas County Circuit Court keep technology dependable for judges, staff, and the communities you serve.

Thank you for your consideration.

Best regards,  
Terry J. Mork
