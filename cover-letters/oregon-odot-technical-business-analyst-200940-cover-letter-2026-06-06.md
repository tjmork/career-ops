**Tim Ghormley, Program Modernization & Business Operations Manager,**

I am applying for the **Technical Business Analyst** role (Operations & Policy Analyst 2) in ODOT’s **Commerce and Compliance Division**. I am an **Oregon resident**, a **U.S. Army National Guard veteran**, and drawn to public-service work where clear systems, accurate data, and understandable policy communication help Oregonians safely use our transportation network.

My career sits at the intersection of **business analysis, technical systems, and cross-functional delivery**—a strong match for CCD’s modernization and operations mission. I hold a **BS in Computer Information Systems** and more than **ten years** of experience bridging business needs and technical execution. Below I address the qualities listed under **“What we’d like to see.”**

**Analyze complex business processes, systems, and operational data.** As **Business Systems Analyst at Columbia Sportswear**, I partnered with engineering to deliver SFCC features across Product, Pricing, Promotions, and Browse & Content—translating merchandising rules into implementable requirements and coordinating integrations (Order Dynamics, Channel Advisor, Bazaarvoice, analytics). At Bath & Body Works I led modernization initiatives including **SiteGenesis to PWA Kit**, **SFCC to SFMC email migration**, and **Canada BOPIS across 114 stores**—each requiring process mapping, data validation, and operational readiness.

**Translate technical and policy information for diverse audiences.** I have written **technical documentation and training materials** (10+ documents and videos at C-LOGIC), explained integration constraints to business partners, and served as **primary client liaison** for an at-risk B2B account at National Data Systems. I am comfortable turning complex system behavior into plain-language guidance for managers, staff, and external partners.

**Support implementations, process improvements, and cross-functional coordination.** I have coordinated division-scale initiatives with Product, engineering, operations, and vendors under deadline pressure—sprint planning, release sequencing, acceptance criteria, and post-launch support. I am accustomed to fast-paced environments where priorities shift and stakeholders need timely, accurate status.

**Data analysis, reporting, and actionable insights.** I work regularly with **SQL**, operational metrics, and analytics platforms (Splunk, Adobe Analytics, Datadog, ServiceNow). I use **Excel** for analysis, reconciliation, and stakeholder reporting. **Power BI has not been my primary visualization tool**, but I am proficient at synthesizing operational data into decision-ready summaries and am prepared to expand Power BI use within CCD standards.

**Relationships, competing priorities, and continuous improvement.** As technical lead for about **five engineers** at Bath & Body Works, I balanced customer, loyalty, and data priorities while mentoring peers and improving logging, documentation, and API standards. I contribute best in collaborative teams that value respectful communication, measurable outcomes, and iterative improvement.

I would welcome a conversation about how CCD approaches **system stewardship**, **policy and training materials**, and **program modernization**—and how I can help the division deliver dependable business systems for motor-carrier compliance and public safety.

Thank you for your consideration.

Best regards,  
Terry J. Mork
